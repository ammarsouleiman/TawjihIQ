Design a complete professional mobile app UI/UX in Figma for an AI-powered academic and career guidance application called “TawjihIQ”.

TawjihIQ helps students choose the best university major and career path based on their personality, academic strengths, skills, interests, goals, and job market demand.

Create a polished, startup-quality, production-ready Figma prototype. The design should feel premium, trustworthy, intelligent, modern, and suitable for high school students, university students, fresh graduates, and parents.

Design requirements:

* Platform: Mobile app
* Device frame: iPhone 15 Pro
* Language: English first
* Must support future Arabic RTL layout
* Include a language switcher: English / Arabic
* Visual style: premium, clean, modern, academic, smart
* Color palette: deep navy, white, soft blue, subtle purple accents
* Typography: modern sans-serif, clear hierarchy
* Components: reusable buttons, inputs, cards, chips, tabs, sliders, progress bars, modals, empty states, loading states, error states
* Use Auto Layout for all screens
* Use consistent spacing system
* Use realistic dummy data
* Use clean illustrations or placeholder illustration areas
* Make the design look like a serious funded startup MVP, not a basic demo

Branding:

* App name: TawjihIQ
* Tagline: “Your AI guide to the right major and future career”
* Logo concept: modern wordmark with an abstract compass, path, or AI spark icon
* Tone: smart, supportive, trustworthy, academic, friendly
